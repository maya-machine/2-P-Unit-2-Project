You Should Travel To... The Ocean!

Not only is the ocean the best place to live because of food, community of fish people, and the healing powers of the ocean, it is also a great place to die. You don't have to worry about going to heaven or hell, because everyone is granted eternal happiness. Forget about simple pleasures like TV and Popcorn, and live in bliss.

But as with most places that are abundant with culture and community, the ocean is neglected by the world. If you travel not too far, you will experience the worlds forgotten dumpster. Visit it for a day to see the damage humanity has done. Don't be shy!
